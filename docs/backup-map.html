Today and after

Live copies through Syncthing

Unchanged by the merge. Every host keeps its copy under /Storage/Files/<folder>. These copies are for access, not history: a deleted or overwritten file stays in Syncthing's trash for 7 days.

What each step changes

  • Step 5, pushed, waiting on Heimdall's deploy: Odin's disk and Ragnarok back up all of /Storage.
  • Step 6: Heimdall backs up all of /Storage. Its Vorta repository is deleted only after that copy lands and passes a check.
  • Step 7: Ragnarok's Vorta history is replayed into its borgmatic repository, keeping the long history.
  • Step 8: the remaining Vorta repositories are deleted, each only once its history is replayed and checked.

Reading the diagrams

  • Each host box holds that host's data and the repositories stored on its disk.
  • solid arrow: a backup that runs on a schedule. "SSH" means it crosses to another machine.
  • dotted arrow: Vorta's backups, stopped on 2026-10-27; those repositories are frozen.
  • thick arrow: history copied from one repository into another.
  • Cylinders are borg repositories; boxes are folders.
  • Every backup skips /Storage/Files/Backups, Steam game files, caches, and Torrents (it has a .nobackup marker).

Syncthing folders

  • All hosts keep them under /Storage/Files/<name>.
  • Most folders go to Odin, Heimdall, Ragnarok and the phone. Projects and Frigate: Odin and Heimdall only. Torrents: Odin, Heimdall, Ragnarok.
  • Heimdall's own server backup does not cover /Storage/Files; Odin's backups are where those files get history.
